What this can and cannot show
  • The forecast, intervals and decision rule were fixed before launch and hashed. Nothing is refitted afterwards.
  • Only Steam is measured. Console GTA V and GTA Online, where switching to GTA VI is most direct, are not visible.
  • The baseline starts Aug 20, 2026, after pre-orders and the August leaks. The test measures the extra change at launch, not the whole effect of the hype.
  • A GTA V discount or GTA Online event timed with the launch is part of the measured gap and cannot be separated from it.
  • The control index removes Steam-wide swings only. Control games are also exposed to GTA VI, so the adjusted gap may understate a fall.
  • Daily backtests cover Sep 30 to Nov 15 and contain no holiday. December rises have ranged widely, so only large December effects are detectable.
